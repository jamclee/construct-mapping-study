About this apartment

A bright one-bedroom on the third floor of a well-kept walk-up, facing south over a quiet residential street. The living room gets good light through most of the day and the bedroom comfortably fits a queen bed with room for a dresser and a small desk.

The kitchen was updated three years ago and includes a full-size refrigerator, a gas range, and a dishwasher. There is a reasonable amount of cabinet space and a small pantry cupboard beside the fridge. The bathroom has a shower-tub combination and a linen closet.

Hardwood floors run throughout the unit apart from the bathroom, which is tiled. Windows were replaced building-wide two winters ago, which has made a noticeable difference to both noise and heating.

Building and neighbourhood

The building has twelve units and an on-site superintendent who handles day-to-day maintenance. Entry is by key fob and there is a secure package room off the lobby. Bicycle storage is available in the basement on a first-come basis.

The block is largely residential and quiet in the evenings. A grocery store, pharmacy, and several cafes are within a five-minute walk, and the nearest bus stop is at the corner. The subway is about twelve minutes on foot, with a second line another five minutes beyond that.

Street parking is available by residential permit. The building does not have dedicated parking spaces, though a paid lot operates two blocks north for tenants who need a reserved spot.

Additional terms

Prospective tenants should be aware of the following before applying. The security deposit is equal to one month's rent and is due at signing, together with the first month's rent.

  • Renter's insurance is required and must be maintained for the term.
  • In addition to the monthly rent, tenants are responsible for a mandatory building assessment of $185 per month, billed separately, which covers water, heating, and common-area maintenance.
  • Cats are permitted with a one-time $150 fee. Dogs are not permitted.
  • Subletting requires written approval from the building manager.

Viewing and application

Viewings are held on weekday evenings and Saturday mornings by appointment. Applications are reviewed in the order received and typically take two to three business days to process.

Applicants should be prepared to provide proof of income, one form of photo identification, and a reference from a previous landlord where available.
