How the introductory rate works

The 0% introductory rate applies to balances you transfer to this account within the first 60 days after it is opened. Transfers requested after day 60 are charged at your standard transfer APR from the date they post, even if the introductory period has not ended.

The introductory period runs for fifteen consecutive billing cycles, beginning with the first full cycle after your account is opened. It is counted in billing cycles rather than calendar months, so the exact end date depends on when in the month your account opens. Your statement will show the remaining number of introductory cycles each month.

When the introductory period ends, any balance still outstanding begins accruing interest at your standard transfer APR from that point forward. Interest is not applied retroactively to the introductory period.

How interest is calculated

Interest is calculated using the average daily balance method, including new transactions. Each day, the balance in each category is adjusted for payments, credits and new charges, and the daily periodic rate is applied to that figure. The results are added together at the end of the cycle.

Because the variable rates are tied to a published index, they may rise or fall over the life of the account. A change to the index takes effect with the first billing cycle beginning after the change is published. We will notify you in writing of any change that is not driven by the index.

Purchases are eligible for an interest-free grace period if you pay your statement balance in full by the due date each cycle. Transferred balances and cash advances are not eligible for a grace period, and carrying a transferred balance may mean new purchases begin accruing interest immediately.

Making payments

Your minimum payment each cycle is the greater of $35 or 2% of your statement balance, plus any interest and fees assessed that cycle, plus any amount past due. Payments are due on the same calendar day each month, and you may change your due date once per year.

Payments up to your minimum are applied to the balance carrying the lowest APR first. Any amount you pay above the minimum is applied to the balance carrying the highest APR first. While the introductory rate is in effect, this means minimum payments will generally reduce your transferred balance rather than any higher-rate balance on the account.

Paying only the minimum will not clear a transferred balance before the introductory period ends. A schedule showing how long repayment would take at the minimum payment appears on each monthly statement.

Fees

The following fees apply to this account. They are charged to your account and appear on the statement for the cycle in which they are assessed.

  • Annual fee: none, for the life of the account.
  • Balance transfer fee: 5% of the amount of each transfer, with a minimum of $15. This fee is charged at the time the transfer is made and is added to your transferred balance.
  • Cash advance fee: 5% of the amount of each advance, minimum $10.
  • Foreign transaction fee: 3% of each transaction in a foreign currency.
  • Returned payment fee: up to $40.

If you pay late or exceed your limit

A late payment fee of up to $40 may be assessed if we do not receive at least your minimum payment by the due date. We do not charge a fee for exceeding your credit limit, though transactions that would take you over it may be declined.

If a payment is more than 60 days late, we may apply a penalty APR of up to 29.99% to all balances on the account, including any balance still within the introductory period. The penalty rate applies indefinitely, though we will review the account after six consecutive on-time payments.

Changes to your account

We may change the terms of this agreement, including rates and fees, with 45 days' written notice. You may reject certain changes by notifying us before the effective date, in which case the account will be closed to new transactions and you may repay the existing balance under the prior terms.

Credit limits are reviewed periodically and may be increased or decreased based on account performance and information from consumer reporting agencies. You may request a limit review once every six months.
